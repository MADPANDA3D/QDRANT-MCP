# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, or technical
preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction and impact.
- Protect privacy: do not publish credentials, tenant data, collection content, private endpoints,
  or personal information.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, attribution, and license obligations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential disclosure, deliberate
service disruption, and sustained bad-faith participation are not acceptable in project spaces or
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, the project, or its users.

Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for platform-wide abuse. For project-specific conduct
concerns, contact the maintainers through the private contact path on the
[MADPANDA3D organization profile](https://github.com/MADPANDA3D). Do not put sensitive evidence in a
public issue. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as the available information permits;
no fixed response SLA is promised.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and
public conduct while representing this project.
